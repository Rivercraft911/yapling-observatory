## Source and modifications

These files come from [Kevin MacLeod's official Kerbal Space Program collection](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/most/kerbalspaceprogram.php)
and its [original ZIP download](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/most/kspost.zip).
Only the three credited tracks are distributed here.

Original MP3 member bytes were extracted from the author's archive. There was
no re-encoding, editing or alteration of their embedded tags. Runtime filenames
were normalized. [Asset provenance](asset-provenance.json) records original
archive member names, ISRCs, source and license URLs, byte counts and SHA-256
identities. Licensing was verified against the author's current track and
licensing pages on 2026-10-26; older embedded tags, if any, were preserved unchanged.

The application source-code license does not replace the music license.
Kerbal Space Program identifies the author's source collection; these credits
do not imply affiliation with or endorsement by the game or its publishers.
