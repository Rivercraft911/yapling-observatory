Spacecraft runtime concept models
Published derivatives reviewed 2026-10-26

These five self-contained GLB exports were independently authored for Yapling
Observatory from parametric Blender geometry. The project maintainer authorized
public distribution of these runtime visuals and supplied the embedded SSI/SATS
artwork. The model exports retain their original bytes; manifest.json records
each filename, revision, byte count and SHA-256 identity.

spacecraft-v03-dipole.glb       v03              Straight dipole exterior
spacecraft-v03-turnstile.glb    v03              Turnstile exterior
spacecraft-v03-v45.glb          v03              Upward V tape exterior
spacecraft-v04-r04-cutaway.glb  v04-r04          Side-service cutaway study
spacecraft-v03-outward-v.glb    v03-outward-01   Outward V tape exterior study

The v03 exterior was built from authored primitives and the supplied SATS
triangulation. The outward V and cutaway are authored derivatives of that
exterior. Manufacturer drawings, the Stanford Sapling range-test photograph,
and OreSat tracker material were inspected as dimensional or appearance
references. No supplier CAD, OreSat board artwork, reference photographs or
reference-image textures are included in these runtime exports. Original CAD,
source artwork, builders and research records remain outside this distribution.

Units are metres. Body +Y is patch-forward and +Z is top. Exported glTF
coordinates are [x_body, z_body, -y_body], with Y up and patch-forward -Z.
Petal animation, where present, is an authored hinge sweep. Geometry and
materials are appearance concepts, with no verified flight fit, tolerances,
deployment dynamics, RF pattern, optical coverage or selected flight hardware.
Rendering must not supply numerical properties to the engineering models.

SSI/SATS and Stanford names and marks remain subject to their owners' rights.
Their inclusion identifies the supplied team artwork; it does not imply
